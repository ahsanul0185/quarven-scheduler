# Quarven Scheduler
### Product Requirements (v2)

A simple web app that builds a weekly staff schedule.

## 1. What it is

A website where a manager uploads a few files, clicks one button, and gets a full weekly work schedule on screen. It shows who works which shift, what task they do, which team they are on, which floor they are on, and when they take a break. Any problems are listed clearly.

Everything runs in the web browser. There is no login, no server, and no database.

## 2. Who it is for

The scheduling manager at a facilities company. They are not a technical person. They should be able to make a full week's schedule in under two minutes.

## 3. The problem

Right now schedules are made by hand in spreadsheets. This causes:

- Two shifts booked for the same person at the same time.
- People not getting enough rest between shifts.
- Shifts that do not have enough staff.
- No clear view of who is doing which task or on which floor.

The app fixes all of this automatically.

## 4. How it works (3 steps)

1. Upload three files: employees, shifts, and tasks. Sample files can load with one click.
2. Click "Generate Schedule". The app works out the best plan using the rules.
3. View and export. See the schedule on the dashboard, then download it as a file.

## 5. The files you upload

All files are CSV or JSON.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Employees | id, name, site, role, skills, and free/busy for each day (available / unavailable / partial). Partial = short shift only (6 hours or less). |
| Shifts | id, site, role, day, start time, end time, min staff, max staff. |
| Tasks (new) | id, which shift, task name, floor, role needed, skill needed, how many people needed. |
| Config | the rule settings (see next section). |

## 6. The rules it follows

The app never breaks these rules when it builds the schedule:

- A person is only given a task if their role and skill match.
- A person is never scheduled on a day they are not available.
- On a partial day, a person only gets a shift of 6 hours or less.
- Everyone gets at least 10 hours rest between shifts.
- No one works more than 48 hours in a week.
- No one works more than 5 days in a row.
- Long shifts (over 6 hours) get an automatic 30-minute break.

You can change these numbers in the config file. If a shift cannot be fully staffed, the app records it as one of four clear problem types: understaffed, not enough rest, too many weekly hours, or too many days in a row.

## 7. What the dashboard shows

- **Overview** — quick numbers: total shifts, staffed, understaffed, coverage %, problems.
- **Weekly grid** — the whole week, colour-coded, with task, floor, time, and break.
- **Floor view** — who is on each floor.
- **Tasks & teams** — each task, its team, and its floor.
- **Breaks** — each person's break times.
- **Problems** — the full list of anything that could not be scheduled.

## 8. What you can download

- The schedule as a CSV file.
- A clean report (HTML/PDF) you can print or share.
- The problem list as a CSV file.

The report uses a dark blue header (#2C3E50) with white text, light grey stripes on the rows, the title "Weekly Roster Report", and a footer "Generated by Quarven Scheduler" with the date.

## 9. What it is built with

- React and TypeScript (the web app).
- Tailwind CSS (the look).
- Recharts (the charts).
- PapaParse (reading the files).

No server, no database, no login, no outside services.

## 10. What it will NOT do

- No login or user accounts.
- No saving data online — you upload fresh files each time.
- No email, payroll, or history.
- No manual drag-and-drop editing of the schedule.
